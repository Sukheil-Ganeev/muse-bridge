## Reliability Rules

- Open-source algorithm structure is evidence.
- Exact production weights, account-specific graph, and current ranking behavior are not fully known.
- Growth claims from third-party playbooks are hints, not guarantees.
- Never present a score as a guaranteed prediction.
- Do not recommend spam, forced engagement, automation abuse, or unsafe account behavior.
