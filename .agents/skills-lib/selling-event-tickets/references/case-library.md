# Case Library

## Case 1 — UFC Shanghai real-name

**Problem:** multiple tickets, per-account limit, no transfer/name change, seats assigned later.  
**Lesson:** obtain final guest passports before purchase; buy directly on each guest; separate orders risk split seating; ask official/B2B channel for a group solution.

## Case 2 — Rolex Shanghai Masters Category S

**Problem:** AI initially confused another Shanghai event and Row S vs Category S.  
**Lesson:** verify exact sport/event/date; category and row are different; distinguish Finals/Day/Night/All Day and hospitality products.

## Case 3 — The Weeknd / secondary + travel

**Problem:** a buyer guarantee does not cover flight/hotel loss if seller fails later.  
**Lesson:** request exact seller/allocation verification before purchase; inspect delivery date and ticket-in-hand status.

## Case 4 — F1 Abu Dhabi group and hospitality

**Problem:** active category pages do not prove 11 or 30 adjacent tickets.  
**Lesson:** check ticket limits, direct allocation/hold and block configuration; preserve earlier quote when viable.

## Case 5 — Official Platinum

**Problem:** expensive dynamic ticket was described like VIP.  
**Lesson:** use exact product name but never add hospitality inclusions.

## Case 6 — Guns N’ Roses PA4

**Problem:** PA4 looked like a VIP category.  
**Lesson:** a section can contain standard and VIP inventory; section is not package.

## Case 7 — Dakar accommodation/VAT

**Problem:** seven-day program showed fewer nights; VAT was initially assumed from event country.  
**Lesson:** request day-by-day accommodation and exact tax treatment from the invoicing entity.

## Case 8 — Ed Sheeran advertising product

**Problem:** need a reusable product pack, dynamic prices and official vs secondary market comparison.  
**Lesson:** prepare clean map, category ladder, age, transfer/resale rules, market benchmark and price-on-request advertising.

## Case 9 — NCT 127 sales not open

**Problem:** partner could be encouraged to wait and buy directly.  
**Lesson:** keep official timing internal; record the lead and return after a defined recheck date.

## Case 10 — Partner margin layers

**Problem:** Marsel, Orhan/Ruzi/other intermediary margins and platform fees could be mixed.  
**Lesson:** model each layer separately and show the final recipient-specific selling price.
