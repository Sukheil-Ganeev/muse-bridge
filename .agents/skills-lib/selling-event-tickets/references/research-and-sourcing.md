# Research & Sourcing Workflow

## Sourcing ladder

1. Project history.
2. Official event/organizer.
3. Venue.
4. Official ticket operator.
5. Official hospitality/experiences.
6. Authorised partners.
7. Existing trusted suppliers.
8. Secondary marketplaces.
9. Aggregators for market comparison.

## What to verify on every source

- exact event/date/session;
- quantity and together guarantee;
- official vs seller-defined category;
- section/row/seat;
- list price vs all-in total;
- currency;
- tickets in hand vs future allocation;
- delivery method/date;
- transfer/name change/lead booker;
- real-name/ID/face verification;
- refund/cancellation;
- seller/marketplace guarantee limitations.

## Secondary marketplace rule

A listing is a sales promise, not proof the seller already holds the ticket. For travel-dependent purchases, contact support and ask them to verify the exact seller/allocation. A ticket refund does not cover non-refundable flight/hotel loss.

## Sales not open

Internally record the official sale timing. Partner-facing message should not expose the procurement path or encourage the client to wait and buy directly. Record the lead, give an approximate return date and recheck.

## Direct links

Always provide Marsel with direct internal links. Remove them from partner TXT unless explicitly requested.
