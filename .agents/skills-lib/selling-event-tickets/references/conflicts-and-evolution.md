# Conflict & Evolution Log

## 1. 35% / 40% / 50% / 60% / fixed profit

**Resolution:** these are event/client/category-specific decisions, not global rules. The AI must recover the latest local instruction. If absent, present scenarios and market comparison.

## 2. AED/USD 3.6725 vs 3.65

Earlier answers used official/reference rates. Later Marsel explicitly established 3.65 as the working commercial rate. Use 3.65 unless a task gives another rate.

## 3. 5% bank reserve

Earlier cases used 5%, 10%, 20 SGD or 100 AED. The final broad instruction is 5% for non-AED purchase. AED purchases do not receive automatic FX reserve; confirmed card/processing fees still apply.

## 4. Full price list vs three selected options

Both are valid:
- direct customer request: curate 3–5 best options;
- advertising/inventory pack: show full ladder;
- partner who needs to add margin: one clear unit price per category.

## 5. Links always vs links never

- Internal Marsel block: always provide direct links.
- Partner/client TXT: remove procurement links unless explicitly requested.

## 6. Mention official sale timing vs hide it

- Internal tracker: record sale/presale timing precisely.
- Partner message: do not reveal procurement strategy or encourage self-purchase; give a recheck date and retain the lead.

## 7. Fixed advertising price vs live quote

- Specific live enquiry: give a final unit quote with reconfirmation.
- Public advertising: preferably price on request because inventory moves.

## 8. “Official Platinum” wording

Keep the exact ticket product name if useful, but never present it as hospitality or promise VIP benefits unless separately included.

## 9. “2 together” and exact seats

Use exact seats only when visible/confirmed. “Together” may be guaranteed without seat numbers; state exactly that.

## 10. VAT assumptions

Never infer VAT from event country. Confirm seller entity, rate and taxable base.

## 11. Partner confidentiality vs deception

Protect supplier and margin data, but do not invent official status, secured stock or guarantees. Use truthful sales language with minimal necessary disclosure.

## 12. Profit terminology

Use “estimated/gross profit” until all costs are known. “Net” only after actual fees, bank cost, supplier cost and delivery are settled.

## 13. One-day/three-day/four-day access

Use product-specific access days, not event weekend dates. A 4-day event can contain a 3-day hospitality product.

## 14. Event map

A public source map may contain face-value prices. Create/send a clean version for partners; retain source map internally.

## 15. Old price vs new market

Price continuity is preferred, but not at a loss. Keep old price when economically viable; otherwise document the reason for change.
