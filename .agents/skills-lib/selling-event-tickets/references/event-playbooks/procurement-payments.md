# Procurement Payments — Decision Framework

This section came from the dedicated debit-card discussion. All bank facts are volatile and must be rechecked.

## Evaluate any account/card by
- debit vs credit/charge;
- single and daily e-commerce limit;
- international merchant acceptance;
- AED-only conversion cost;
- multi-currency wallets and spread;
- cashback/rewards cap and exclusions;
- Aani/local transfer limit;
- new-beneficiary cooling period;
- ATM limit;
- card sharing/supplementary/virtual card rules;
- account ownership and KYC;
- large transaction pre-authorisation and fraud controls.

## Business lesson
A 1% reward is irrelevant if the FX cost is 3%+. For high-value tickets, payment reliability, limit and conversion spread matter more than lounge/cinema benefits.
