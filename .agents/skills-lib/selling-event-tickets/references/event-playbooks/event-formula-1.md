# Event Playbook — Formula 1

## Product ladder
GA/Hill → Grandstand → Club → local hospitality → Champions Club/Yas Suites → Paddock Club → Team Suite → F1 Garage/ultra-VIP.

## Mandatory fields
- exact Grand Prix and year;
- event days vs product access days;
- seat/view/turn;
- reserved/free seating;
- food and drinks, including alcohol;
- indoor/outdoor/climate controlled;
- parking/entrance;
- concerts and whether Golden Circle upgrade is separate;
- paddock/pit-lane/grid/trophy experiences;
- guest age;
- appearances subject to change.

## Large group control
Ticket limit per order can be lower than the group size. Confirm 11/30-seat allocation directly; do not infer from an active page. Plan blocks and adjacent rows.

## Pricing
Hospitality amounts are large: even a low percentage creates high absolute profit, but payment risk is also high. Use exact full cost and supplier cancellation liability.
