# Playbook — Advertising Inventory

## Deliverables per event
1. one-page product overview;
2. price ladder or price-on-request instruction;
3. clean category map;
4. age and entry rules;
5. transfer/delivery rules;
6. VIP/hospitality ladder;
7. short selling points;
8. media assets that do not expose procurement prices;
9. lead intake form;
10. recheck date/status.

## Lead intake fields
Event, date/session, quantity, adult/child ages, category, seats together, budget, VIP preference, travel dates and contact.

## Price policy
For volatile inventory, advertising should not promise a fixed price. Use “available upon request / subject to availability”, then issue a live quote.
