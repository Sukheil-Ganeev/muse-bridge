# Platform Playbooks

> This section records **operational lessons from the chats**, not timeless platform policy. Reverify current terms before every purchase.

## Ticketmaster / Etihad Arena

- Often official UAE channel.
- Per-ticket/per-order fees may appear at checkout.
- `Official Platinum` is pricing/seat inventory, not hospitality.
- Delivery/transfer varies by event; do not generalise Yas Island App/mobile transfer rules.
- Venue may warn against unauthorised resale.

## StubHub

- Displayed price may be all-in in some markets; verify final checkout.
- Listing can be “not in hand”; ask delivery date and confirmed allocation.
- “Seated together” does not reveal exact seat numbers.
- Check organiser resale restrictions and transfer method.

## Viagogo

- Similar seller-fulfilment/delayed-delivery risk.
- Do not rely only on buyer guarantee when flights/hotel depend on the ticket.
- Confirm currency, all-in total, physical/FedEx/mobile delivery and seller proof.

## Ticombo

- Marketplace/service fee can materially change the total.
- Category-only inventory may not guarantee exact section/row.
- Verify seller, transfer, all-in checkout and number together.

## Platinumlist

- Common official/regional source.
- Service/processing fee may be event-specific.
- Transfer through app may exist only if enabled for the event.
- Standing/VIP age restrictions can differ.

## Webook / Alawwal Park

- Official Saudi sales can appear as TBC/non-clickable before release.
- Secondary labels Category 1/2/3/VIP are not automatically official stadium categories.
- Confirm ticket transfer/membership-card mechanics.

## JUSS Sports / Damai / Migu / Trip.com (China)

- JUSS was the key sports/event channel in Shanghai cases.
- Damai is broad Chinese ticketing; UI/language can be difficult.
- Migu may provide fan-club/presale access.
- Trip.com can be more accessible to foreigners and may offer bundles.
- For strict real-name events: final guest passport, per-ID limits, no transfer and late seat allocation can apply.

## F1 Experiences / Formula 1 Tickets

- Official premium/hospitality source.
- Product names may have 3-day vs 4-day access differences.
- Confirm paddock tour, pit lane, team appearance, food/drink, seat/view and age for the exact package.

## MotoGP Premier

- Processing fee may be shown separately and can be calculated precisely.
- Package experiences may be guaranteed, registration-based or first-come-first-served; preserve the wording.

## ATP Experiences / JUSS — Shanghai Masters

- ATP House is a single-day hospitality product in the analysed case.
- Category S is a ticket category, not Row S.
- Day/Night/All Day and court products must be separated.

## Livepass Argentina

- In the analysed Ed Sheeran case: service fee, dynamic QR, app transfer and resale restrictions were central.
- International card rules and validation can be event-specific.
- Reverify all current details before using.

## HK Ticketing / Cityline / All Ticket / ThaiTicketMajor

- Asian/K-pop delivery can be physical, app, collection or real-name.
- Presale timing, membership rules, age/height and transfer must be checked for the exact show.

## Ticketon

- In the analysed Kazakhstan case, a service fee and non-refundable policy affected full cost.
- Never reuse the old percentage without current checkout.

## FSO Motorsport / Dakar suppliers

- Need day-by-day accommodation, exact nights, hotel/camp category, room configuration, VAT and cancellation.
- Public package price may exclude tax; confirm seller entity and B2B treatment.

## Grandstand Tickets

- Treated in the chats as a ticketing agent using suppliers, not an official tournament operator.
- Payment may precede final supplier confirmation; ask whether inventory is already secured or sourcing starts after payment.

## SeatPick / Gigsberg / Biletwise / other aggregators

- Useful for market discovery and category vocabulary.
- Not automatically an official or low-risk procurement channel.
- Follow through to the actual seller, checkout and transfer rules.
