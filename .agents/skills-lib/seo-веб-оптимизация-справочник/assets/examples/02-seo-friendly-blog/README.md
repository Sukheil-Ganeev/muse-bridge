# SEO-Friendly Blog Article Example

### Article Metadata
- **Word Count:** 1,680 words
- **Reading Time:** 7-8 minutes
- **Target Keyword:** "best time to visit dubai"
- **Long-tail Keywords:**
  - best time to visit dubai desert safari
  - when to visit dubai weather
  - dubai weather by month
  - desert safari best season
- **Search Intent:** Informational (user researching when to visit Dubai)
- **Target Audience:** Tourists planning UAE vacation

### SEO Structure

#### Title Tag (H1)
"Complete Guide: Best Time to Visit Dubai for Desert Safari - Weather, Season & Tips"
- Length: 78 characters (exceeds recommended 60, but keyword-rich)
- Primary keyword at beginning
- Emotional trigger (Complete Guide)
- Specific benefit (Best Time)

#### Meta Description
"Discover the best time to visit Dubai for desert safari. Expert guide on weather, seasons, prices, and insider tips. Plan your perfect UAE vacation in 2024-2025."
- Length: 156 characters
- Contains primary keyword
- Compelling CTA (Plan your perfect vacation)
- Specific timeframe (2024-2025)

#### URL Structure
`/blog/best-time-visit-dubai-desert-safari/`
- Descriptive and keyword-rich
- Lowercase (canonical)
- Hyphens for word separation
- No special characters
