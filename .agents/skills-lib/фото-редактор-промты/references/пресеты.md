## Пресет 1: LinkedIn Professional

**Интенсивность: 2/10 | Риск: Минимальный**

```
Professional LinkedIn profile photo enhancement - Trustworthy Executive Look.

OBJECTIVE: Create a professional, approachable appearance that inspires confidence and trust. The person must look exactly like themselves - just well-lit, rested, and competent.

APPLY (SUBTLE - intensity 2/10):
- Balance lighting across the face evenly
- Reduce harsh shadows under eyes by 20-30% (not eliminate)
- Even out skin tone slightly while keeping all texture visible
- Remove ONLY temporary blemishes (fresh pimples, irritation)
- Improve overall image clarity and sharpness
- Ensure background is clean and non-distracting

PROTECTION PROTOCOL (DO NOT MODIFY):
- Face shape and width - preserve exactly
- All facial proportions - nose, lips, eyes, jaw
- Skin texture - keep all natural pores visible
- Expression lines and character wrinkles - they show experience
- Eye size, shape, and color
- Any permanent features (moles, freckles, scars)
- Natural asymmetry - this is identity

STYLE REFERENCE:
- Target: Modern corporate headshot
- Quality: Professional studio lighting simulation
- Mood: Confident, approachable, trustworthy
- NOT: Glamour magazine, NOT: Beauty filter, NOT: Passport photo

QUALITY STANDARDS:
- Must pass "video call test" - look identical on Zoom/Teams
- HR professionals should see no obvious editing
- Colleagues who know this person should recognize instantly
- Enhancement should be completely invisible

IF IN DOUBT: Preserve over enhance
WHEN UNCERTAIN: Do less, not more
FALLBACK: If any change would alter recognition - skip that change entirely

Result: "This person looks professional and well-rested" - not "This photo is edited"
```

**Когда использовать:** Профиль LinkedIn, корпоративный email, сайт компании, networking
**Fallback:** При любых сомнениях - только улучшить освещение

```
Corporate CV/Resume photo enhancement - Conservative Professional Standard.

STRICT PROTECTION (DO NOT TOUCH):
- Facial structure and bone proportions
- All permanent identifying features
- Natural skin texture including pores
- Age-appropriate expression lines
- Eye characteristics (size, shape, depth)
- Natural facial asymmetry
- Unique identifying marks (moles, beauty marks)

QUALITY CHECKPOINTS:
[ ] Person recognizable at arm's length from CV
[ ] No "magazine quality" that seems out of place
[ ] Skin has visible human texture
[ ] Appropriate for serious professional context
[ ] Would pass scrutiny of experienced HR professionals

PRIORITY: Authenticity > Perfection
```

```
- Blemishes, marks, moles, scars
- Wrinkles or expression lines
- Hair color, texture, or position
- Eye color or appearance
- Lip color or shape
- ANYTHING about the person's appearance
=== END PROHIBITION ===

BACKGROUND ONLY EDITS:
If background needs correction:
- Make uniform as required (white/light gray/light blue)
- Remove shadows on background
- Do NOT alter the person's outline or edge
- Natural transition between person and background

QUALITY VERIFICATION:
Before output, verify:
[ ] Face is 100% identical to input
[ ] All identifying features preserved
[ ] Skin texture unchanged
[ ] All marks/moles present
[ ] No smoothing applied anywhere on person
[ ] Only technical image quality improved

OUTPUT STANDARD:
Photo must pass:
- Automatic biometric verification systems
- Human document officer inspection
- Border control facial recognition
- Direct comparison with subject in person

IF IN DOUBT: Do not make the change
WHEN UNCERTAIN: Leave as original
PRIORITY: Document validity > Photo aesthetics
```

**Когда использовать:** Паспорт, виза, ID, водительские права

## Пресет 8: Quick Fix

**Интенсивность: 2/10 | Риск: Минимальный**

```
Quick safe photo fix - Minimal Risk Enhancement.

OBJECTIVE: Make this photo slightly better without any risk of negative outcomes. When in doubt, don't change. Safety first.

PHILOSOPHY: "Better to under-edit than over-edit"

APPLY (ULTRA-SAFE - intensity 2/10):

ALWAYS SAFE:
- Lighting balance improvement
- Color temperature correction
- Exposure adjustment
- Basic contrast optimization
- Image sharpness (subtle)
- Noise reduction

CAUTIOUSLY SAFE (minimal):
- Very slight skin tone evening
- Remove only obvious temporary blemishes
- Minor under-eye shadow reduction (20% max)

NEVER CHANGE:
- Any facial features
- Face or body shape
- Age appearance
- Permanent marks/features
- Skin texture (keep all of it)
- Anything you're not sure about

DECISION RULE:
For each potential change, ask:
"Could this cause any problem?"
If yes or maybe -> Don't do it
If clearly no -> Proceed carefully

SAFE OUTPUT CHECKLIST:
[ ] Photo looks like same person
[ ] No visible editing artifacts
[ ] Natural appearance maintained
[ ] Could be original photo with better lighting
[ ] No one would question if this is edited

USE CASES FOR THIS PRESET:
- When you need quick improvement
- When you're not sure what level is appropriate
- When the photo will be seen by people who know you
- When you need "good enough" fast
- As a starting point before deciding if more is needed

THIS IS NOT FOR:
- Professional headshots (use Headshot Studio)
- Dating apps where you want to look best (use Dating Attractive)
- Executive portraits (use Executive Portrait)
- When you have time for careful editing

SPEED VS QUALITY:
This preset prioritizes SAFETY over ENHANCEMENT
You get: Safe, quick improvement
You don't get: Dramatic transformation

IF IN DOUBT: Do nothing to that element
```

**Когда использовать:** Быстрое улучшение, неуверенность в уровне, фото для знакомых
**Fallback:** Только баланс освещения

## Пресет 12: Children Safe

**Интенсивность: 1/10 | Риск: МИНИМАЛЬНЫЙ**

```
Children's photo enhancement - MINIMAL INTERVENTION ONLY

=== SPECIAL PROTECTION PROTOCOL ===
Children's photos require MAXIMUM restraint.
Children are perfect as they are.
"Improvement" of children's features is inappropriate.
=== END PROTOCOL ===

OBJECTIVE: Technical improvement ONLY. Preserve the child's natural appearance completely. These photos document childhood - authenticity is paramount.

STRICTLY ALLOWED (intensity 1/10):

TECHNICAL ONLY:
- Lighting balance (fix harsh shadows)
- Color correction (accurate skin tones)
- Exposure adjustment
- Noise reduction
- Red-eye removal (flash artifact)
- Crop adjustment if needed

MINIMAL ALLOWED:
- Remove fresh blemish (mosquito bite, temporary scratch)
- Fix obvious photo artifact (lens flare, etc.)

=== ABSOLUTE PROHIBITIONS ===
- ANY facial feature modification
- Skin smoothing of any kind
- Eye enhancement or brightening
- Teeth whitening
- "Beautification" of any type
- Hair smoothing or perfecting
- ANY change that makes child look "different"
=== END PROHIBITIONS ===

WHY MINIMAL:
- Children should be documented as they are
- Baby teeth, messy hair, gap-toothed smiles = precious memories
- "Flaws" in childhood photos become cherished later
- Parents want real memories, not edited ones
- Future comparison with other photos should match

PRECIOUS IMPERFECTIONS TO PRESERVE:
- Baby teeth (crooked, missing, gaps)
- Messy hair (action shots, wind, play)
- Natural skin (childhood freckles, baby cheeks)
- Authentic expressions (silly faces are memories!)
- Real moments (food on face, grass stains)

DOCUMENTATION STANDARD:
These photos may be viewed:
- At graduation -> "Look how little you were!"
- At wedding -> "You haven't changed!"
- With their own children -> "You looked just like this"

RESULT STANDARD:
Photo looks like professional camera + good lighting captured this moment.
NOT: Photo looks like this child was "enhanced."

IF IN DOUBT: Make NO changes
PRIORITY: Authenticity is everything
```

```
- Comparison between subjects

- Same editing intensity for ALL faces
```
