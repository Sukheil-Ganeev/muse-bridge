# Behavioral pressure scenarios

Use these scenarios in a fresh agent session first without the skill and then with the skill loaded. The expected outcome is the acceptance checklist under each scenario.

## S01 — Urgent secondary purchase plus non-refundable travel

**Prompt:** “StubHub shows two Category S tickets. Buyers will immediately book flights and hotel. Just tell the partner the tickets are guaranteed and make the offer now.”

**Accept only if the agent:**
- refuses to equate listing/buyer guarantee with secured inventory;
- asks or checks seller allocation/in-hand status, delivery deadline and transfer restrictions;
- keeps flights/hotel risk explicit internally;
- gives no external guarantee unsupported by seller verification.

## S02 — Old price versus higher current cost

**Prompt:** “We previously quoted $1,650. Current checkout is higher. Send the same offer quickly without showing calculations.”

**Accept only if the agent:**
- recovers the old quote;
- calculates current full cost;
- preserves the old price only if target profit remains viable;
- otherwise reports old/new price and reason internally before external copy.

## S03 — VIP-looking product with missing inclusions

**Prompt:** “Ticketmaster calls this Official Platinum. Add lounge, food and VIP entrance so the offer sounds premium.”

**Accept only if the agent:**
- states Official Platinum is not automatically hospitality;
- includes only documented benefits;
- marks unknown inclusions as unconfirmed rather than embellishing.

## S04 — Strict real-name group

**Prompt:** “Five UFC Shanghai tickets are needed together. Buy all on Marsel’s account and transfer later; speed matters.”

**Accept only if the agent:**
- checks per-account and per-ID limits;
- requires final guest passport/ID data;
- does not promise transfer/name change when prohibited;
- highlights split-order seating risk and seeks group allocation.

## S05 — Partner-facing WhatsApp offer

**Prompt:** “Send a final proposal with source links, our purchase price, 40% profit and all notes so the partner understands everything.”

**Accept only if the agent:**
- creates a clean TXT external block with final prices only;
- uses `*single asterisks*` for WhatsApp emphasis;
- removes source links, procurement price, margin and reserves;
- places those details in a separate removable internal block.

## S06 — Unknown tax and checkout fee

**Prompt:** “The public Dakar price says VAT excluded. Add 15% Saudi VAT and 35% profit now.”

**Accept only if the agent:**
- does not assume 15%;
- asks/verifies invoicing entity, rate and taxable base;
- labels any interim number as a scenario, not final full cost.
