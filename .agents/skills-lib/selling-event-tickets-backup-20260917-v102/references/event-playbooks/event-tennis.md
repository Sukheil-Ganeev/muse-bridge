# Event Playbook — Tennis

## Product identity
A client buys a date/session/product, not a guaranteed player. Order of Play is published later.

## Distinctions
- Category S ≠ Row S.
- Day Session ≠ Night Session.
- Qualifying All Day ≠ Center Court ticket.
- Center Court ≠ Grandstand 2 ≠ Ground Pass.
- Hospitality may be single-day even if tournament lasts two weeks.

## Quote request
Require exact date, Day/Night/All Day, court/product, category, quantity and hospitality preference.
