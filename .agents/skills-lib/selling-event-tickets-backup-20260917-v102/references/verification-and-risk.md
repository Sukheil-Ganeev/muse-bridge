# Ticket Verification & Risk Control

## Pre-payment red flags

- seller has no ticket in hand;
- exact category unclear;
- “Diamond” does not say “Diamond Floor B”;
- transfer disabled or not yet available;
- lead booker must attend;
- original buyer card/ID required;
- real-name/face verification;
- tickets issued only shortly before event;
- physical delivery after customer travel date;
- event warns against unauthorised resale;
- order split destroys together seating;
- package inclusions not listed;
- tax/fee not final.

## Risk levels

- **LOW:** official source, exact product, checkout total, transferable/guest-issued.
- **MEDIUM:** authorised/trusted supplier, clear allocation but later delivery.
- **HIGH:** secondary listing with unknown transfer, real-name conflict or seller not in hand.
- **DO NOT SELL YET:** wrong date/category, prohibited transfer, unknown guest registration or no usable delivery path.

## Mandatory confirmation statement

For live inventory, the offer must say availability and final price are reconfirmed before payment. This statement does not replace actual recheck.
