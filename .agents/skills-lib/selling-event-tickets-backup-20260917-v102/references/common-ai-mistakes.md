# Common AI Mistakes — DO NOT REPEAT

1. Start a new search without checking prior quotes.
2. Mix two similarly named events.
3. Quote procurement price as selling price.
4. Add profit before fees.
5. Call an internal reserve a seller fee.
6. Double-count all-in marketplace fees.
7. Use a stale exchange rate or wrong currency symbol.
8. Apply another event’s markup automatically.
9. Change an old partner price without explanation.
10. Call Official Platinum a VIP package.
11. Promise food, alcohol, parking, entrance or lounge without product proof.
12. Treat a venue suite as event inventory.
13. Turn “Diamond” into “Diamond Floor B”.
14. Invent row/seat numbers.
15. Promise seats together because quantity = 2.
16. Ignore per-order ticket limits for groups.
17. Buy a real-name ticket on Marsel and expect later transfer.
18. Ignore lead-booker/original-ID/face verification.
19. Give a refund guarantee as if it covered flights/hotel.
20. Tell the partner exactly where to buy directly.
21. Put supplier links, procurement and profit in partner TXT.
22. Write overly formal AI-like copy with excessive emojis.
23. Use `**bold**` instead of `*bold*` for WhatsApp.
24. Omit age rules.
25. Omit current UFC card or forget “subject to change”.
26. Assume exact VAT rate.
27. Repeat a full catalogue after the client chose one package.
28. Promise current availability based only on an active page.
29. Present a future sale date as current inventory.
30. Publish a fixed advertising price for a highly volatile product without approval.
31. Call estimated profit “net”.
32. Forget optional add-ons are optional.
33. Confuse event days with hospitality access days.
34. Promise a player will appear in a tennis session before Order of Play.
35. Treat a secondary seller category as an official stadium category.
