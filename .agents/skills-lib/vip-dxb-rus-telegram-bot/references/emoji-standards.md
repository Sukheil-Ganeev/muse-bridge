## Emoji Usage Rules

### DO:
- ✅ Use one emoji per button/service
- ✅ Match emoji to service type
- ✅ Be consistent across same category
- ✅ Use emoji at start of button text
- ✅ Combine max 2 emoji for combo offers

### DON'T:
- ❌ Use emoji in middle of text
- ❌ Mix different emoji styles
- ❌ Use emoji in variable names
- ❌ Overuse emoji (more than 2 per line)
- ❌ Use country flags (except UAE context)

## Accessibility Notes

- Emoji render differently on iOS/Android
- Some emoji may not display on older devices
- Always include text description after emoji
- Don't rely on emoji alone for critical info
