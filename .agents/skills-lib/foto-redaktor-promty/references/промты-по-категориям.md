# Библиотека промтов по категориям

> 16 категорий редактирования с промтами разной интенсивности.
> Каждый промт включает ACTION, PROTECTION, STYLE и FALLBACK.
> Краткий список категорий — в SKILL.md.

---

## Структура каждого промта

```
ACTION     - что конкретно делать
PROTECTION - что НЕ менять (критически важно)
STYLE      - желаемый стиль результата
FALLBACK   - что делать если не получается
```

## 1. ГЛАЗА

### Защитный блок для глаз (включать всегда)
```
EYE PROTECTION PROTOCOL:
- Eye size: EXACTLY unchanged (not even 1% larger)
- Eye shape: EXACTLY unchanged
- Eye spacing: EXACTLY unchanged
- Pupil size: maintain current dilation
- Iris color: preserve exact hue and saturation
- Natural blood vessels in sclera: keep 30%+
- Catchlight position: preserve or match light direction
```

### 1.1 Убрать тёмные круги (лёгкая коррекция)

**Интенсивность: 2/10**

```
ACTION: Slightly brighten the dark circles under the eyes by 15-20%.

METHOD:
- Subtle lifting of shadow area beneath eyes
- Blend gradually into surrounding skin
- Keep natural transition to cheek area

PROTECTION (CRITICAL):
- Eye color, size, shape - NO changes
- Skin texture in under-eye area - keep all pores
- Natural shadow gradient near nose bridge
- Tear duct pink coloring
- Eyelash definition

AVOID:
- Complete shadow removal (looks flat)
- Affecting the eye whites
- Creating unnaturally bright patches
- Plastic-smooth under-eye area

STYLE: Person looks well-rested, not retouched.

FALLBACK: If cannot brighten without losing texture, only adjust overall exposure.
```

---

### 1.2 Убрать тёмные круги (умеренная коррекция)

**Интенсивность: 4/10**

```
ACTION: Reduce dark circles under the eyes by 30-40%.
Maintain natural appearance with some depth remaining.

METHOD:
- Reduce shadow intensity while preserving eye socket structure
- Color-correct any blue/purple discoloration
- Blend seamlessly with surrounding skin tones

PROTECTION (CRITICAL):
- Eye size and shape - PIXEL-PERFECT to original
- Natural skin texture with visible pores
- Subtle transition between cheek and under-eye area
- Natural hollow shape of eye socket

FORBIDDEN:
- Completely flat under-eye area
- Bright white patches
- Losing 3D facial contour
- Plastic-smooth texture

STYLE: Like photographed after 8 hours of sleep, not Photoshopped.

FALLBACK: If texture is lost, reduce correction to 20% and preserve texture.
```

### 1.4 Добавить блеск глазам (catchlight)

**Интенсивность: 3/10**

```
ACTION: Enhance existing catchlight OR add natural-looking catchlight
if absent.

SPECIFICATIONS FOR NEW CATCHLIGHT:
- Position: upper portion of iris (10-11 o'clock or 1-2 o'clock)
- Shape: soft rectangle or organic shape (NOT perfect circle)
- Size: approximately 15-20% of iris diameter
- Opacity: 70-85% (not pure white)

CRITICAL RULES:
- Both eyes must have matching catchlights
- Position must match apparent light direction in photo
- Edges should be slightly soft, not hard
- Must look like genuine light reflection

PROTECTION:
- Eye size: NO change (measure before/after)
- Eye shape: NO change
- Iris pattern and texture: preserve completely
- Pupil size: unchanged

DO NOT:
- Create multiple catchlights (unless second light source visible)
- Place catchlight in center of pupil
- Make catchlight larger than natural
- Add catchlight that contradicts shadow direction

STYLE: Eyes look more alive, like good studio lighting.

FALLBACK: If catchlight looks artificial, only brighten existing reflections by 10%.
```

### 3.3 Симуляция студийного 3-точечного света

**Интенсивность: 5/10**

```
ACTION: Simulate three-point studio lighting setup.

CONFIGURATION:
1. KEY LIGHT (main): 45 degrees to side, slightly above
   - Brightest source
   - Creates main shadows

2. FILL LIGHT: Opposite side, lower intensity
   - Fills shadows from key
   - 50% intensity of key

3. RIM/HAIR LIGHT: Behind subject
   - Creates edge highlight
   - Separates from background

IMPLEMENT:
- Main illumination on one side of face
- Softer fill on opposite side (30% darker)
- Subtle rim light on hair/shoulders

CRITICAL:
- All three lights must be consistent with each other
- Shadows must make physical sense
- Catchlight should reflect main light position
- Skin texture must be preserved throughout

RESULT SHOULD LOOK:
- Professional studio portrait
- Clear dimension and separation
- Natural despite being "constructed"

FALLBACK: If inconsistent, simplify to main + fill only.
```
