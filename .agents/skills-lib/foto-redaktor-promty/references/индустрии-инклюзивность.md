## ИНДУСТРИИ: СПЕЦИАЛИЗИРОВАННЫЕ ПРЕСЕТЫ

### Медицина (Healthcare)

**Приоритеты:** Доверие, компетентность, чистота
**Особенность:** Возраст = опыт = доверие пациентов

```
HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL PORTRAIT

APPLY:
- Clean, trustworthy appearance
- Professional lighting
- Remove fatigue only (long shifts)

CRITICAL:
- KEEP age markers (experience = trust)
- Clean but not glamorous
- Competent, not model

Patients should think: "I trust this doctor"
```

## ИНКЛЮЗИВНОСТЬ: ВАЖНЫЕ ПРОТОКОЛЫ

### Защита от AI Bias

**Проблема:** AI модели могут иметь bias против определённых тонов кожи, возрастов, типов внешности.

**Симптомы bias:**
- Осветление тёмной кожи
- "Европеизация" черт лица
- Омоложение как "улучшение"
- Сужение носа/губ
- "Универсальный стандарт красоты"

**Защитные меры:**
```
ANTI-BIAS PROTOCOL:

1. SKIN TONE: Preserve EXACT original tone
   - No lightening
   - No desaturation
   - Keep all undertones

2. FEATURES: No "normalization"
   - Nose shape: unchanged
   - Lip fullness: unchanged
   - Eye shape: unchanged

3. CULTURAL MARKERS: Respect
   - Natural hair textures
   - Cultural beauty standards
   - Regional aesthetic preferences
```

### Региональные стандарты красоты

#### Азия (East/Southeast)
```
ASIAN BEAUTY STANDARDS AWARENESS:

PROTECT:
- Natural eye shape (no westernization)
- Skin undertones (often yellow/warm)
- Face shape characteristics

AVOID:
- Adding crease to monolid
- Enlarging eyes "Western style"
- Changing nose bridge

RESPECT: Asian beauty ideals may differ from Western.
```

#### Ближний Восток (Middle East)
```
MIDDLE EASTERN BEAUTY STANDARDS:

PROTECT:
- Rich olive/warm undertones
- Strong features (eyebrows, nose)
- Natural hair texture

AVOID:
- Lightening skin
- Thinning eyebrows
- Reducing feature prominence

RESPECT: Different standard of beauty and elegance.
```

#### Африка (Africa)
```
AFRICAN BEAUTY STANDARDS:

PROTECT:
- Full range of dark skin tones
- Natural hair textures
- Feature diversity

AVOID:
- ANY lightening
- "Softening" features
- Imposing one standard

RESPECT: Enormous diversity across continent.
```

#### Латинская Америка (Latin America)
```
LATIN AMERICAN DIVERSITY:

PROTECT:
- Wide range of skin tones
- Mixed heritage features
- Regional variations

AVOID:
- Homogenizing appearance
- Single "Latin look"
- Ignoring regional differences

RESPECT: Incredible ethnic and cultural diversity.
```
