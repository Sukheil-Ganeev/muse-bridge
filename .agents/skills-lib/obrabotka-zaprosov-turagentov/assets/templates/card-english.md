# Product Card Template (English)

## Purpose
Card for sending to **expats and foreign clients**. Contains ONLY recommended prices in English.

## What to show
- Recommended prices (Recommended Rate)
- Product description
- Terms and conditions

## What NOT to show
- B2B prices (wholesale)
- Agent profit
- Commissions

## Formatting rules (WhatsApp)
- Bold: `*text*`
- Separator: 15 dashes `───────────────`
- 2 spaces after category
- Emojis only: 💰 ➕ ⚠️

---
